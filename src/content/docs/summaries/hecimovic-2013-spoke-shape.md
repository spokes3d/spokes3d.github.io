## What these notes use it for

The explanation of spoke shape ([Q5](/questions/)): a spoke is diffuse for targets with a low
second ionization potential (Ti, Nb) and triangular with a sharp trailing edge for a high one
(Al, Cu, Mo, Cr). Only doubly charged metal ions (and Ar⁺) can release a secondary electron, and
gas rarefaction removes the Ar⁺. See [Measured properties](/experiments/).
