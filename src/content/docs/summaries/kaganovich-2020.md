## Why it matters here

It is a review of partially magnetized E×B plasmas, the family that Hall thrusters and magnetron
spokes belong to, and so a source of reference cases and of work on the azimuthal instabilities
that a PIC code must reproduce (see [Charoy et al. 2019](/summaries/charoy-2019/)).
