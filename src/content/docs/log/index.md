## 2026-10-27

- [Paper overview](/resources/papers/) added: one table of all papers with type, status, the
  questions and model rungs each bears on, and citation count; it can be filtered and sorted.
  Counts are filled in by `npm run papers:update`, and weekly by a workflow.
- Added the [reading queue](/resources/reading-queue/), a [timeline](/resources/timeline/) of papers
  per year and type, a [BibTeX export](/papers.bib), and the [numbers to match](/experiments/numbers/).
- [Anatomy of a magnetron](/resources/magnetron-anatomy/): a clickable cross-section and a
  description of each part and plasma region, with what matters for spokes.
- Stub summaries for Kaganovich et al. 2020 and Hecimovic et al. 2013.

## 2026-10-21

- Second aim added: reproduce the published magnetron simulations, one model family at a time.
- Ionization region models added to the [simulation families](/simulations/) and to the
  [model ladder](/simulations/model-ladder/) as rung 1d. First case:
  [Gudmundsson et al. 2022](/summaries/gudmundsson-2022-irm-copper/), copper.
- These notes published as a site.

## 2026-10-15

- First versions of the 0D global model, the drift-fluid model, the neutral gas dynamics and the
  Charoy benchmark set-up.

## 2026-10-14

- Programme started, built on the review by Hecimovic and von Keudell (2018) and on the gaps it
  names.
