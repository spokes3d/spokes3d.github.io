**Back-attraction probability** ($\beta_t$)
: The share of the ionized sputtered atoms that the cathode potential pulls back to the target
  instead of letting them leave towards the substrate.

**Critical ionization velocity**
: $\sqrt{2eU/m}$, the speed at which a neutral atom's kinetic energy equals its ionization
  energy $eU$. Spoke speeds in HiPIMS are of the same order.

**dcMS**
: Direct-current magnetron sputtering, the low-power steady mode.

**Drift-fluid model**
: A fluid model in which the electron velocity across the magnetic field follows from the drift
  approximation (E×B, diamagnetic and collisional drifts) instead of a momentum equation.

**E×B drift**
: The drift of electrons perpendicular to both the electric and magnetic fields, at speed
  $E/B$. In a magnetron it runs azimuthally along the racetrack, at ~100 km/s.

**Global (0D) model**
: A model of densities and temperatures averaged over a volume, with losses through its
  boundaries. It can be time-resolved but has no spatial structure.

**Gradient-drift instability**
: The generalisation of the Simon–Hoh instability, driven by density and magnetic-field gradients
  in a plasma with an electric field across the magnetic field. The leading linear candidate for
  spoke onset.

**HiPIMS**
: High power impulse magnetron sputtering: short pulses (tens to hundreds of µs) at peak power
  densities orders of magnitude above dcMS, which ionize much of the sputtered metal.

**Ionization probability** ($\alpha_t$)
: The share of the sputtered atoms that are ionized in the ionization region.

**Ionization region (IR)**
: The dense, bright plasma close to the target over the racetrack, where most of the ionization
  happens.

**Ionized flux fraction**
: The share of ions in the flux of sputtered material reaching the substrate.

**Mode number**
: The number of spokes around the racetrack at one time.

**PIC-MCC**
: Particle-in-cell with Monte Carlo collisions: the plasma is represented by computational
  particles moving in self-consistent fields, colliding with a neutral background.

**Racetrack**
: The closed erosion track on the target, under the region where the magnetic field is parallel
  to the target surface.

**Rarefaction**
: The depletion of the working gas in front of the target during a pulse, by the sputter wind,
  ionization and heating.

**Retrograde rotation**
: Rotation against the E×B direction, seen for spokes at low power.

**Self-sputtering**
: Sputtering of the target by ions of its own material, which can sustain a discharge with
  little or no working gas.

**Spoke**
: A localized ionization zone that rotates along the racetrack.

**Sputter wind**
: The momentum the sputtered atoms carry away from the target, which pushes the working gas out
  of the ionization region.
