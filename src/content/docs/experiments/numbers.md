The numbers from [Measured properties](/experiments/), gathered for quick comparison. Nothing
here is new: each row is taken from that page or from a [summary](/summaries/), and
**[R*n*]** is reference *n* of Hecimovic and von Keudell 2018. Conditions are those of the
2-inch circular planar magnetron unless stated. Check the source before quoting a value.
