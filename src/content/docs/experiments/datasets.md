## For global models

The ionization region model is driven by the measured discharge current and voltage waveforms.
For the copper cases of [Gudmundsson et al. 2022](/summaries/gudmundsson-2022-irm-copper/), these
exist only as figures in the paper. They have to be digitized, or requested from the authors,
before the cases can be rerun.
